AWS Step Functions — PhotoPipelineStateMachine
ModerateContent
Rekognition
Scans for explicit content. Quarantines the photo and stops the pipeline if flagged.
↓
ProcessImage
Pillow · Lambda
Generates thumbnail and preview, applies all 14 photographer adjustments, burns watermark, embeds copyright metadata.
↓
TagImage
Rekognition · Lambda
Extracts GPS and EXIF data, computes a visual fingerprint for duplicate detection, identifies dominant color palette and scene labels.
↓
EnrichWithAI
Bedrock · Claude
Generates a title, description, and keyword tags from the image using AI.
↓
EmbedPhoto
Titan Embeddings
Generates a mathematical fingerprint of the image to power "More like this" similarity search.
↓
NotifyProcessed
EventBridge
Publishes a photo.processed event so downstream services are notified the photo is ready.
⚠
On any stage failure: publishes photo.pipeline.failed to EventBridge and stops execution.
